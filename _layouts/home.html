What the label doesn't tell you

Triple screened

All of our shavings pass through a purpose-built three-part screen. The first 'star screen' removes any oversize pieces, with the remaining shavings entering a rotary trommel screen that extracts the sawdust and fines. The final phase of the screening process removes the smaller airborne dust particles.

Our story

Thirty-odd years of shavings

Breeder's Choice has been packaging woodshavings in Colac since 1994 — a family business on the edge of the Otways, one of Victoria's historic pine-growing regions. It's still run by the same family today.

The shavings start as a by-product of timber milling — kiln-dried radiata pine, nothing else. Everything after that is the business: triple screening, pressing into 14kg bales, robotic palletising and shrink-wrapping. One product, done carefully, for over three decades.

In 2016 the family took on Superior Shavings — now baling from Tumut, in the heart of NSW softwood country, to the same standard for customers north of the border.
