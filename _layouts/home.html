Our story

Thirty-odd years of shavings

Breeder's Choice has been packaging woodshavings in Colac since 1994 — a family business on the edge of the Otways, one of Victoria's historic pine-growing regions. It's still run by the same family today.

The shavings start as a by-product of timber milling — kiln-dried radiata pine, nothing else. Everything after that is the business: trommel screening, dust extraction, pressing into 14kg bales, robotic palletising and shrink-wrapping. One product, done carefully, for over three decades.

In 2021 the family took on Superior Shavings in Tumut, NSW — a second plant in the heart of NSW softwood country, baling to the same standard for customers north of the border.
